How Parallel works
1 · Pick a style

Choose an app from the icon row at the top of the panel.

  • Apps: Messages, Instagram, Discord, X, Facebook and Lock Screen.
  • Instagram opens a second row of modes: DM, Story, Profile, Post and Inbox.
  • Switching apps keeps your people and messages, so you can export the same cast many ways.
  • On a phone, the preview sits above the controls.
2 · Set up the people
  • Rename anyone in the People list. You can have up to four people.
  • Tap an avatar circle to change its colour, or press IMG to upload a photo.
  • Photos are resized to 640px and stay in this browser only.
  • The person marked YOU is the one whose messages appear on the right.
3 · Write the conversation

Add a message with the buttons under the Messages list, then type into it. Each message has these controls:

  • The name button cycles the sender through your people.
  • The emoji button adds a reaction.
  • ↩ turns the message into a reply to an earlier one. Press it again to move the quote, and once more to clear it.
  • ⏱ adds a time divider above the message.
  • IMG attaches a photo.
  • The arrows move the message up or down.
4 · Export
  • Download PNG saves exactly what you see, at 1×, 2× or 3× resolution.
  • The frame height follows the number of messages, so a short chat exports as a short image.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the image opens in a new tab. Press and hold it, then choose Save to Photos.
  • Your work saves in this browser automatically, so closing the tab will not lose it.
5 · X (Twitter)

X has three layouts, chosen in the X panel.

  • Tweet: the first message is the main post, and every message below it shows as a reply. Set the reply, repost, like and view counts on each.
  • Profile: shows that person's posts under the header. The Viewer control switches between This is me, Follow and Following.
  • Handles, the verified badge and the private lock live under each person in the People list.
6 · Instagram Profile & Post
  • Profile shows a full profile with a 3-across grid. Post shows a single feed post.
  • Both read the person picked in the Instagram panel.
  • Add photos, captions, likes, comments, location and a liked-by name under Posts.
  • Posts save in this browser only and do not sync to your account.
7 · Instagram Story & Inbox
  • A Story holds any number of text blocks. Drag a block to move it, drag the bottom-right handle to resize, and the top-right handle to rotate.
  • Each block can be boxed, bold, plain or highlighted, in any swatch colour.
  • Inbox is the DM list with round Notes bubbles on top. Edit the notes in the panel.
8 · Lock Screen
  • An iOS-style lock screen: upload a wallpaper and set the big clock and date.
  • Toggle the lock icon, flashlight and camera, a now-playing music card and a charging indicator.
  • Add notifications from Messages, Instagram, Discord, X, Phone (a missed call) or a custom app.
  • Reorder each notification and edit its title, message and time.

Anything you make here is a fictional mock-up. It is not a real message, post or profile from any real service, and none of these companies are involved with this site.
